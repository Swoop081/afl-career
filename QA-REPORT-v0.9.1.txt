AFL Career v0.9.1 — Simulation QA Report

Automated tests completed:
- 10,000 simulated fixtures (20,000 team scores)
- 20,000 sampled real-player performances
- 10,000 played-match engine score-flow simulations

Results after fixes:
- Average simulated team score: 75 points
- Median simulated team score: 75 points
- 10th–90th percentile: 58–91 points
- Average scoring shots per team: 21.2
- No simulated team score exceeded 160 points in the 10,000-fixture test
- Maximum simulated team goals: 16
- Maximum simulated individual goals: 6 in the off-screen vote model; normal match allocation caps key forwards at 7 and the created player at 8
- Impossible goals greater than disposals: 0
- Sampled elite midfielders averaged 31.6 disposals
- Elite midfielders recorded 35+ disposals in 25.6% of sampled games
- Elite midfielder maximum: 42 disposals
- Played-match flow averaged approximately 68 points per team, with normal variation and occasional high-scoring games

Corrections made:
- Rebalanced simulated scoring-shot totals
- Increased played-match scoring frequency to realistic levels
- Revalidated goals against team totals
- Enforced position-based individual goal ceilings
- Recomputed player impact after final goal distribution
- Enforced goals <= kicks <= disposals consistency
- Updated cache and save migration to v0.9.1

Limitations:
Automated simulation validates match-engine distributions and internal stat consistency. Manual phone testing is still required for touch controls, animation timing, audio, vibration and visual layout on each device/browser.
