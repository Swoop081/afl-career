AFL Career Simulator v0.9.4 QA Report

Implemented
- Added a Selected Teams transition screen before a selected player enters Match Day.
- Goal and snap actions now require possession gained by a mark or clean gather inside forward 50.
- Pressure, spoil, body and blocking actions no longer incorrectly grant possession.
- Added a second shot validation inside kickSlider to prevent direct or stale-state goal attempts.
- Possession source is cleared after disposals, turnovers, scores, quarter changes and new events.
- Existing eight-goal match safety cap remains active.
- Updated app, manifest and service-worker cache version to v0.9.4.

Regression checks
- Weekly selection flow continues to Match Day.
- Omitted/emergency players continue to simulation/reserves choices.
- Marks and gathers still open normal possession actions.
- Defensive contest actions advance play without showing disposal/goal choices.
