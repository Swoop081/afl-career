AFL Career v0.9.2 — Structural Realism QA

Validation performed
- JavaScript syntax validated with Node.js.
- Match-day selection now contains 23 players per club.
- The created player replaces the lowest-rated selected player in the same position group where possible.
- Interactive matches retain continuous background stat accumulation throughout each quarter.
- Final reconciliation only completes remaining team totals and no longer rebuilds every player's game from scratch.
- Goals remain constrained by disposals and kicks.
- Best-on-ground presentation now displays statistics relevant to each position.
- Persistent season records are maintained for played and simulated matches.
- League screen now displays season disposal-average leaders.
- Finals bracket validated as:
  Week 1: 1v4 QF, 2v3 QF, 5v8 EF, 6v7 EF
  Week 2: QF losers v EF winners
  Week 3: QF winners v SF winners
  Week 4: Grand Final
- Qualifying-final winners receive the double chance and advance directly to preliminary finals.
- Elimination-final losers are removed immediately.
- Existing v0.9.1 careers migrate to the new season-stat structure.

Known scope
- This is a structural realism patch. Full weekly selection, reserves, training, contract negotiation, trades and list evolution remain planned for v1.0 and later milestones.
